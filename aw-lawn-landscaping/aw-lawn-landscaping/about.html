Our Story

Built from the Ground Up Since 2017

A&W Lawn and Landscaping started in 2017 with a simple goal: provide dependable, high-quality lawn care that our neighbors could count on. What began as a small local operation has grown into a trusted name in the community — built not through advertising, but through word of mouth and repeat customers who know what to expect.

We're not a franchise or a faceless company. Every job we take on is personal to us — whether it's a weekly lawn cut or a complete landscape redesign. We show up, we do the work, and we stand behind it.
